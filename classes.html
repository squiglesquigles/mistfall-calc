Classes

The six playable Hunter classes. Each is defined by the weapons it can wield and the role it fills — and weapons gate which skills it can use.

Class tier list (community read)

How the classes rank in the current patch, drawn from community coverage and player results. Balance is still settling — see the full tier list for per-context ratings (solo, trio, new players), or tap a class for its complete breakdown.

All classes

Mercenary

A
Bruiser · Sword & Shield / Hammer

The archetypal frontline Hunter — a durable melee bruiser built around sword-and-board and blunt weapons, trading sustained pressure for survivability.

Sorcerer

S
Mage · Staff

A ranged spellcaster that scales with Magical Increase, leaning on the staff to deal burst magical damage from a distance.

Blackarrow

A
Ranged · Bow

A dedicated archer that controls space from afar with the bow, converting positioning and physical damage into safe, sustained DPS.

Shadowstrix

S
Assassin · Dagger / Dual Blades

A high-mobility assassin built for daggers and dual blades — closes distance, opens with critical strikes, and disengages before retaliation lands.

Seer

B
Support · Catalyst / Mace

A utility caster oriented around the catalyst and Healing Bonus, supporting the team through sustain and control rather than raw damage.

Withered Knight

A
Heavy · Greatsword

A heavy two-handed greatsword specialist that trades speed for reach and poise, excelling at toughness-break and parry against tough targets.

Class roles and signature weapons are inferred from in-game weapon-type gating and attribute scaling. Per-class skill and weapon lists are joined live from the catalog; the tier read is a directional take on the current patch, refreshed as the meta settles.
