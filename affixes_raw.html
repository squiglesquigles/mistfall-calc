Affixes

The stat modifiers that roll on gear. All 44 affixes are listed below with the effect each one grants, the levels its bonuses unlock at, and how many weapons and armor pieces can actually roll it — 32 of them can, the rest are defined but appear on no gear or gem in this build.

How affixes scale with level

An affix's level sets a multiplier on top of its base value. The gear ladder is perfectly linear: every affix level adds +1.5%, so a level-10 roll is worth +15% and a level-20 roll is worth +30%. It stops there, though — the bonus caps at +48% at affix level 32, and levels past that (the table runs to 40) add nothing. Wine buffs use the same +1.5% per level, but their ladder ends at level 8 for +12%.
