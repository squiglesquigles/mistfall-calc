Sorcerer build calculator — FAQ

Is the Mistfall Sorcerer build calculator free?

Yes — it is free, needs no sign-up, shows no ads, and runs entirely in your browser. It is an unofficial fan tool by Squigle, not affiliated with Bellring Games.

How does the Sorcerer build calculator find the cheapest build?

It tries every legal combination of Sorcerer gear, gems and sockets against average market prices and returns the cheapest loadout that reaches your chosen affix levels.

Which weapons does the Sorcerer build calculator support?

The Sorcerer build calculator supports Staff. Switch weapons and the whole loadout re-optimises automatically.

Does the Sorcerer build calculator include gem and socket prices?

Yes — gear, gems and sockets are all priced at average market prices, so the total shown covers the full loadout cost.

Why can I not see all Necklaces and Rings?

The calculator shows only the possible combinations of gear plus Necklaces and Rings that can create your build. If you have found a build that includes a specific ring or necklace, please leave some feedback and I will update accordingly.
